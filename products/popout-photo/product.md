A photo in an ordinary frame with its subject standing out in front of the glass and past the frame's
edge. The subject is a 3D-printed silhouette carrying a matching photo sticker, held by a thin tab that
slips between the glass and the frame. RJ's full concept, with 16 example ideas, is in the 3d-modeling
repo: `ideas/series/popout-photos/concept.md` (2026-11-08).

## Sketch (launch scope)

- **Sizes:** 4×6, 5×7 and 8×10 inches, portrait or landscape, in the customer's own frame.
- **Subject:** one, chosen with the subject picker. Several separate pieces is an open question for later.
- **Mounting edge:** top, bottom, left or right, wherever the subject leaves the frame. A tapered tab
  holds it, and a separate shim takes up a loose frame.
- **Enlargement:** a few presets to choose from, with the percentages set by testing.
- **Sticker:** the subject at the silhouette's exact scale, printed by RJ and cut on the Cricut (Print
  Then Cut). RJ applies it, or ships it for the customer to apply.
- **Background print:** the whole photo at the frame's size, printed by RJ or by the customer (question).

## What exists

- The concept notes and one prototype photo: the fisherman, where only the fish pops out.
- lithophane-maker: subject masks (IS-Net), outlines from a mask, extrusion. No tab or shim yet.

## Prototype

The concept's first physical check: a silhouette, a thin tapered tab and a separate shim in several real
frames, then the alignment against a real print and sticker.

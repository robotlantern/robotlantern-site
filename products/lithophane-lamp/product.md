A small lamp whose sides are photo tiles that slide in and out, so the pictures can change with the
season or the year. A few base designs share one tile size, so any tile fits any lamp. It's lit by a
flameless tealight or a USB puck light (RJ's memo, 2026-11-10).

## Sketch

- **Tiles:** flat white lithophanes (the engine's `flat` form), one size and thickness for every base,
  with edges that slide into grooves. Printed upright for continuous tone, or flat at fine layers.
- **Bases:** for example a one-tile desk frame, a four-sided lantern and a six-sided one. Printed in a
  colour with no overhangs, so Slant could print them later. Each holds the light at its centre.
- **Light:** Slant's tealight (SL013, $0.50, warm flicker, coin cell) or USB puck light (SL011, $10,
  brighter; it needs a USB-A charger such as SL071, $2.50). At launch RJ keeps lights in stock himself,
  and buying them is his call.
- **Orders:** a lamp with its first tiles, one photo each, or tiles alone for a lamp someone already
  has. One order can carry several photos (docs/ORDERS.md, `photo.count`).

## What exists

  printed.

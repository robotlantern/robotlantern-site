### What is it, exactly?

Two flat 3D-printed parts in PLA. The stencil, in one colour, is your drawing with its features cut
out. It presses onto a base in a second colour, so the base shows through the cut-outs. There's no
glue: small pegs on the back hold it. A ring at the top hangs it. The optional name tag below is cut
through the same stencil, so its letters show the base colour.

### How big is it?

The drawing fits a box 8 cm wide and 8.5 cm tall (about 3 by 3⅓ inches). The base adds a thin rim,
the ring sits on top, and the name tag adds 1.7 cm below. Hooks and ribbon aren't included.

### Who makes the drawing?

If you add a photo, OpenAI's image model redraws it as a two-colour stencil, which takes about half
a minute to a minute. You get up to three tries per photo and pick the one you like. Each try is
checked on the page, and one that wouldn't print isn't offered. The gag gifts and Appalachian
designs are our own drawings and need no photo.

### What photos work best?

A clear, close, well-lit photo of one face, looking at the camera, with nothing covering it. Crop
to the head as the page suggests. Busy backgrounds are fine, because only the face is drawn.

### Can I order a person, or a child?

Yes, if they've said yes. For a child, you need to be their parent or guardian, or have one's OK.
Use only photos you have the right to use. Studio and school portraits usually need the
photographer's release.

### What won't you make?

Logos, brand marks, cartoon or film characters, other people's artwork, and text in the drawing
(the name goes on the tag). We refund any order we can't or shouldn't make, in full.

### Can my AI assistant help, or can I bring my own drawing?

Yes. An assistant that can make images can draw it to [our drawing spec](drawing-spec.json), and
you upload it under "I have my own drawing". Then your photo never comes to us. You still see the
preview and pay on Stripe's page yourself. See [Ordering with an AI assistant](ai/).

### Can I put several designs in one order?

Yes: up to 12 designs and 50 ornaments, shipped together in one box. Every ornament in the order is
priced by the order's total count, so mixing designs still counts toward the lower price. For more
than 50, write to [help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com) for a quote.

### Will it arrive in time for Christmas?

Order by the date above. We ship within 5 business days of your order, usually sooner, and delivery
takes 2 to 5 business days more. See [Shipping](/shipping/).

### What if it arrives broken, or doesn't look like the preview?

We remake it for free or refund you, whichever you'd rather. Every ornament is built from the
preview you approve and checked against it before it's printed. See
[Returns and refunds](/returns/).

### What happens to my photo?

It goes to OpenAI to make the drawing, and to our order service. If you don't order, it's deleted
after two days. If you do, it's deleted 60 days after your order ships. Slant 3D, which prints and
ships the ornament, never sees it. The details are on the [privacy page](/privacy/).

### Is it safe on the tree?

It's a decoration, not a toy: keep it away from small children and pets that chew. PLA softens in
heat, so keep it off hot bulbs and out of a hot car.

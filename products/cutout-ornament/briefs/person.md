Redraw the person in this photo as a two-colour stencil. If there is more than one person, draw only the one
whose face is largest and nearest the centre, and leave out everyone and everything else.

The image: the head, hair and the top of the neck only (no shoulders, no clothes, no hands, no background
objects), plus anything worn on the head, seen as in the photo, keeping the likeness, face shape, hair and
expression exactly; never change the expression. Square image, plain flat white background, the head filling
about 80% of the width. Exactly two flat colours, no gradients, no shading, no grey, no texture, no text, no
lettering, no tags or name plates.

Style: the head is mostly solid red, and the features (eyes, brows, nose edges, the mouth, teeth as ONE white
shape, hair strands, ear edges, beard edges, and the edges of anything worn on the head) are drawn as white
cut-outs in it, like a bold poster stencil or linocut. This art will be cut from one sheet of red plastic about
80 mm wide and laid on a white base, so:
- All the red is ONE connected piece. No floating red dots, strokes or islands. Where a detail would float,
  join it to its neighbour with a short red bridge, the way real stencils do.
- No red line thinner than 2% of the image width, and no white gap thinner than 2% either. Few, chunky shapes;
  stubble or freckles are a few bold shapes, not dots. Eyes are simple bold shapes, not tiny dots.
- A continuous red outline at least 2.5% of the width thick runs all the way round the outside, so the outer
  edge is one smooth closed shape.

Red is pure #E01010. White is pure #FFFFFF.

Redraw the pet in this photo as a two-colour stencil. If there is more than one animal, draw only the one whose
head is largest and nearest the centre.

The image: the pet's head and the top of the neck only (no body, no collar tags, no background objects), seen
as in the photo, keeping the likeness, markings and expression exactly; never change the expression. Square
image, plain flat white background, the head filling about 80% of the width. Exactly two flat colours, no
gradients, no shading, no grey, no texture, no text, no lettering, no tags or name plates.

Style: the head is mostly solid red, and the features (eyes, nose highlights, ear edges, fur or feather lines,
markings, mouth) are drawn as white cut-outs in it, like a bold poster stencil or linocut. This art will be cut
from one sheet of red plastic about 80 mm wide and laid on a white base, so:
- All the red is ONE connected piece. No floating red dots, strokes or islands. Where a detail would float,
  join it to its neighbour with a short red bridge, the way real stencils do.
- No red line thinner than 2% of the image width, and no white gap thinner than 2% either. Few, chunky shapes.
- A continuous red outline at least 2.5% of the width thick runs all the way round the outside, so the outer
  edge is one smooth closed shape.

Red is pure #E01010. White is pure #FFFFFF.

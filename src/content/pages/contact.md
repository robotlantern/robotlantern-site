Email is the best way to reach us. Robot Lantern is one person, RJ, and email lets him keep track of every
order.

- **About an order:** [help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com). A question before you order,
  a change to one, or a problem with something that arrived.
- **Anything else:** [admin@robotlantern.com](mailto:admin@robotlantern.com). A correction, a question about
  copyright or privacy, or a note about something you read.

[Shipping](/shipping/) and [Returns and refunds](/returns/) may answer your question first.

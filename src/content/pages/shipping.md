Everything in the shop is made to order. RJ makes each piece after you order it, then ships it himself.

## Where we ship

To addresses in the United States only. Checkout won't take an address anywhere else.

## How long it takes

Each product page says how long that piece takes to make. That's its handling time: from your order to
the day it goes in the mail. Delivery time comes on top of that.

RJ checks every photo before he starts. If he needs a different one, he emails you, and the handling time
starts when the new photo arrives.

Some products show an order-by date before a holiday: the last day to order and still leave time to make
and deliver it. Once a package is in the mail, delivery is up to the carrier.

If we can't ship within the handling time, we'll email you before it runs out, with a new date. You can
wait, or cancel for a full refund.

## What it costs

Each product has a flat shipping charge. It's shown on the product page and added at checkout.

## On the way

Orders go by USPS. If yours hasn't arrived when it should have, write to
[help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com). If it's lost, RJ will make another or refund you.
If it arrives damaged, see [Returns and refunds](/returns/).

We ship to the address you give at checkout, so check it there. If it's wrong, write as soon as you can.
We can change it before the order ships, but not after.

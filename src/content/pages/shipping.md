Every ornament is made to order. After you pay, its print files are built from the preview you
approved and checked against it. RJ approves the order, and Slant 3D prints it and ships it to you.

## Where we ship

To addresses in the United States only. Checkout won't take an address anywhere else.

## What it costs

Nothing extra. The price on the product page includes shipping, and an order of several ornaments
ships in one box. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

## How long it takes

- **Making it:** we ship within 5 business days of your order, and usually sooner. That's the time
  to build and check the print files, approve the order, and print it.
- **Delivery:** usually 2 to 5 business days more, depending on where you are.

Before Christmas, the product page shows an order-by date: the last day to order and still leave
time to make and deliver it. Once a package is in the mail, delivery is up to the carrier.

If something stops us shipping within 5 business days, such as a print file that doesn't match
your preview, we email you before the time runs out. You can wait for a new date, or cancel for a
full refund.

## On the way

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. If it hasn't arrived when it should have,
write to [help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com). If it's lost, we'll make another or
refund you, whichever you'd rather. If it arrives broken, see [Returns and refunds](/returns/).

We ship to the address you give at checkout, so check it there. If it's wrong, write straight
away. We can change it until the order goes to print, which is usually within a day, but not after.

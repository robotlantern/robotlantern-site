title: Returns and refunds
description: Pieces made from your photo are final sale, except when they arrive damaged or made wrong.
updated: 2026-11-10
---

Everything in the shop is made for you, from your photo, so it can't be sold to anyone else. That's why
every order is final sale: we can't take a return or make an exchange because you changed your mind.

The exceptions are below. For any of them, write to [help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com).

## If your photo won't work

RJ looks at every order before he makes it. If your photo won't make a good piece, he emails you before
making anything. You can send a different photo, or have a full refund.

## If you change your mind straight away

Write as soon as you can. If RJ hasn't started making your order, we'll cancel it and refund you in full.
Once he's started, it's final.

## If it arrives damaged or made wrong

If your order arrives broken, or isn't what you ordered (the wrong photo or options, or a fault in how it
was made), we'll make it again or refund you, whichever you'd rather.

- Write within 14 days of delivery if you can. Carriers set deadlines for damage claims.
- Send a photo of the problem, and of the box if it was damaged on the way.
- Keep the piece and the box until we've sorted it out, in case the carrier asks to see them. You won't
  need to send anything back unless we ask, and then we pay the postage.

Handmade and 3D-printed pieces vary a little. Fine layer lines, and small differences from the preview on
your screen, are part of how they're made, not faults.

## Refunds

Every refund is for the full amount you paid, shipping and tax included. It goes back to the card you paid
with, through Stripe. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.

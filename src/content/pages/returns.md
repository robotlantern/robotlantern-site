Every ornament is made for you, from your drawing and your choices, so it can't be sold to anyone
else. That's why orders are final sale: we can't take a return or make an exchange because you
changed your mind.

The exceptions are below. For any of them, write to [help@robotlantern.com](mailto:help@robotlantern.com)
with your order number (it's on the thank-you page and in Stripe's receipt).

## Our promise

If your ornament arrives broken, or doesn't match the preview you approved, we'll make it again for
free or refund you, whichever you'd rather.

- Write within 14 days of delivery if you can. Carriers set deadlines for damage claims.
- Send a photo of the problem, and of the box if it was damaged on the way.
- Keep the ornament and the box until we've sorted it out, in case the carrier asks to see them.
  You won't need to send anything back unless we ask, and then we pay the postage.

3D-printed pieces vary a little. Fine layer lines, the small tidying the preview warns about, and
colours that differ slightly from your screen are part of how they're made, not faults.

## If it can't be made as you approved it

Every order is checked before it's printed. If the print files don't match your preview, we email
you before printing anything. You can have a fresh drawing from the same photo, or a full refund.

## If you change your mind straight away

Write as soon as you can. If your order hasn't gone to print yet, which usually happens within a
day, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once it's printing, it's final.

## If it's lost in the post

We'll make another or refund you, whichever you'd rather. See [Shipping](/shipping/).

## Refunds

Every refund is for the full amount you paid, tax included. It goes back to the card you paid with,
through Stripe. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.

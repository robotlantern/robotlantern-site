# Email

Every address is an alias of one Google Workspace mailbox that RJ reads (RJ, 2026-11-22). Aliases scope the
work and make it easy to filter, so use the one that matches the job. Up to 30 can be made; ask RJ before
assuming a new one exists. Personal and account addresses are listed in `.private/email.md`, not here.

| Address | For | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `help@robotlantern.com` | Customer support, and replies about an order ("Needs you" follow-ups, refunds, fresh drawings) | Site pages, FAQ, policies; RJ |
| `orders@robotlantern.com` | Order operations: the contact email on each Slant draft, and the routines' emails to RJ ("Ready for Slant", "Needs you", "Robot Lantern daily") | The order Worker (docs/ORDERS.md), `rl-orders` and `rl-daily` (docs/PRODUCTION.md) |
| `signup@robotlantern.com` | "Coming soon" interest emails (decision 9). Nothing is stored | Product pages for lines that aren't open |
| `info@robotlantern.com` | General enquiries | Not used on the site yet |

The routines send only to `orders@` and never to customers. Customer shipping emails are Gmail drafts that
RJ sends himself (decision 23).

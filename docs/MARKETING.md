# Marketing the photo products

Planned 2026-11-10 (NOW.md). The launch centres on four product lines, and posts on other platforms point
at them. A product's kit is made in its listing step, once its prototype has passed.

## A kit per product

Drafts go in `.private/marketing/<slug>/` until they're posted. Listing images go on the site, under
`public/images/shop/<slug>/`.

- **Hero:** the real prototype, lit if it lights up. One square (1080 × 1080) and one tall (1080 × 1920).
- **How it works,** in three pictures: the photo, the screen where you shape it, the finished piece in a
  hand.
- **A 15–30 second video:** the configurator on a phone, the print, the reveal. For a lithophane, the
  reveal is the light coming on. Meltbot's camera can catch the print (3d-modeling,
  `tools/printer/camsnap.py`).
- **Examples:** three to six, each a real print made from an example photo. For pop-outs, the concept's
  shortlist: fisherman, beach soccer, jumping dog, mountain biker, wedding.
- **Words:** three captions, alt text for every picture, and the product's counted short link
  (`robotlantern.com/y/<slug>`, built before the first post; docs/LOG.md, task 17).
- **Dates:** order-by dates for seasonal products, repeated in posts as they get close.

The agent says which shots it needs in the product's file, and RJ takes them in his prototype step.

## Rules (NOW.md decision 10)

- Listings show real prototypes, or renders marked as renders. No AI pictures that redraw the product:
  lithophane-maker's GPT-Image lifestyle shots are concept art, and the faces in them aren't the print's.
- Example photos are RJ's own, family's with their OK, or AI-made stand-ins marked as such where they
  appear. A stand-in is fine as an input, because the print shown is what the product really makes of
  it. Anyone recognisable in a photo agrees first, or a parent agrees for a child.
- A customer's photo or order is never shown without the customer's written OK.
- Shop links in posts are the counted short links, never tracking links. The privacy page says how they
  count (one sentence).
- Build pages carry no shop links: they use ESA data, which is non-commercial (docs/LOG.md, task 15).

## Platforms (NOW.md decision 12)

Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest first: personalised gifts sell from pictures, and Pinterest is where
people plan gifts. Each one gets the tall image and the video; Pinterest gets the hero and the examples
as pins linking to the product page.

- **Black Friday to Cyber Monday (11-27 to 11-30):** every line that's open, and "coming soon" for the

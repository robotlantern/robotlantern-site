Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Robot Lantern Uploader (the “App”) is a private tool operated by Robot Lantern LLC. It exists for one purpose: uploading videos, using the YouTube API Services, to YouTube channels owned by the person who runs it. It is not offered to the public, and it has no accounts, sign-ups, or customers.

What the App accesses

When its operator signs in with Google, the App asks for a single permission: the YouTube upload scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). That permission lets the App upload videos and set their title, description, and visibility on the channel the operator chose. It does not let the App read your watch history, comments, subscriptions, or private videos.

What is stored

The App stores an OAuth refresh token so it can upload without asking the operator to sign in each time. That token is kept in a restricted file on the operator’s own computer. No other Google user data is collected, copied, or retained by the App.

Sharing

Robot Lantern LLC does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train any machine-learning model. The only data the App sends anywhere is the video and its details, to YouTube, at the operator’s direction.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke the App’s access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Questions: admin@robotlantern.com.
